# FAQs

## 1. How quickly can I get a service?
Services are assigned based on availability in your area, usually within a short time frame.

## 2. How are professionals verified?
All professionals go through a screening process to ensure basic skill and reliability. Further they are trained as per the working protocol of AfixZ.

## 3. How is pricing decided?
Pricing is kept transparent and is shared before the service begins. It is decided based on the time, involvement, availability of accessories, and detailing of the work.

## 4. Do you provide follow-up after service?
Yes. We check to ensure the issue has been properly resolved. In fact, follow-ups are our USP and we leave no customer unsatisfied at any cost.

## 5. What if the issue is not fully fixed?
You can report it, and we’ll arrange a follow-up to address it.

## 6. Do I need to provide tools or materials?
Most professionals carry basic and specialised tools. Additional materials, if needed, will be informed beforehand or arranged accordingly as per the requirements.

## 7. How can I trust the professional visiting my home?
We assign verified professionals and maintain service records for accountability. We also make sure to take before and after pictures as a record to assess the quality of service.

## Design Context

### Users
Urban homeowners and renters, 25–40 years old, Delhi NCR (Delhi, Noida, Gurgaon). Time-poor, phone-first, rely on word-of-mouth and brand trust for home service decisions. They've been burned by unreliable contractors before. They want confidence, not just convenience.

### Brand Personality
**Reliable · Premium-but-affordable · Accountable**

AfixZ is not the cheapest option — it's the trustworthy one. The brand sits between "local guy from JustDial" (unreliable, stressful) and "Urban Company" (polished, maybe overpriced). AfixZ's value proposition: vetted professionals, guaranteed work, fair pricing.

Tone: Confident. Direct. Grounded. No corporate fluff. No startup-speak. Speaks like a skilled craftsman — not many words, but every word earns its place.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Reference**: Urban Company — clean, professional, app-like. But AfixZ needs more *personality*. Urban Company feels sterile. AfixZ should feel skilled and human.

**Theme**: Light mode. This is a consumer product used on phones in living rooms and offices during the day.

**Visual tone**: Editorial precision meets workshop craft. Think a well-designed trade magazine — clean grids, strong typography, purposeful accent color (the orange #f36b21 is expressive and should be used confidently, not timidly as a button color alone).

**Anti-references**: No glassmorphism. No purple-to-blue gradients. No "feature grid with icon above heading" pattern. No AI-generic dashboards.

### Brand Tokens
- Primary (dark navy): `#1f2933`
- Accent (orange): `#f36b21`
- Accent light: `#ffb37d`
- Body font: Roboto Condensed (clean, slightly condensed — industrial feel)
- Heading font: Raleway (geometric, clean)
- Display font: Cormorant Garamond (used sparingly for editorial pull quotes)

### Design Principles
1. **Confidence over softness** — big statements, not hedged copy. The brand is assertive about its quality.
2. **Orange earns its place** — the accent color should punctuate, not decorate. Use it like an ink stamp, not a highlighter.
3. **Craft, not tech** — avoid glass effects, gradients, and visual effects that feel like SaaS. Think printed matter, not app store screenshots.
4. **Hierarchy through contrast, not cards** — avoid wrapping everything in rounded rectangles. Use size, weight, whitespace, and line dividers instead.
5. **Readable at speed** — users are on phones, skimming. Short punchy sentences. Bold numbers. Clear CTAs.
